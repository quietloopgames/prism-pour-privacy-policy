Prism Pour

Privacy Policy by QuiteLoopGames

Effective date: September 10, 2026

Prism Pour is developed and published by QuiteLoopGames. This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use the Prism Pour Android application.

Information collection

The current version of Prism Pour does not require an account and does not collect, transmit, sell, rent, or share personal information with QuiteLoopGames or third parties.

Information stored on your device

Prism Pour stores the following gameplay information locally on your device:

  • Level progress and star ratings
  • Unlocked collections
  • Daily challenge progress and streaks
  • Sound, music, and haptic settings
  • Other gameplay preferences

This information remains on your device and can be deleted by clearing the app's storage or uninstalling the application.

Device permissions

Prism Pour does not access your location, contacts, photographs, camera, microphone, or personal files. The application includes Internet permission to support optional online functionality and service availability. Core gameplay remains available without creating an account.

Advertising and purchases

The current version does not serve third-party advertisements and does not process real-money in-app purchases. If advertising, purchases, cloud accounts, or other online services are introduced in a future version, this Privacy Policy and the Google Play Data Safety declaration will be updated before that version is released.

Children's privacy

Prism Pour is intended for a general teen and adult audience and is not directed primarily at children under 13. The current version does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Data retention and deletion

Locally stored gameplay information remains on the device until the user clears the application's storage or uninstalls it. Because the current version does not create online accounts or store personal information on servers controlled by QuiteLoopGames, there is no server-based personal account data to retain or delete.

Security

QuiteLoopGames takes reasonable measures to design Prism Pour so that gameplay information remains local to the user's device. However, no device or electronic system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

Changes to this policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when Prism Pour's features or data practices change. The updated policy will be published on this page with a revised effective date.

Contact

For privacy questions or support, contact quiteloopgames@gmail.com.
