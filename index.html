Prism Pour

Privacy Policy by quiteloopgames

Effective date: September 19, 2026

Prism Pour is developed and published by quiteloopgames. This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use Prism Pour on Android or iOS.

Information you provide

You can play Prism Pour as a guest without creating an account. On supported Apple devices, Sign in with Apple is optional and may provide Prism Pour with an Apple account identifier and, only when you choose to share them, your name or email address. This information is used only to provide account and progress-saving features.

Gameplay and cloud progress

Prism Pour stores gameplay information such as level progress, star ratings, unlocked collections, challenge progress, streaks, purchases, and sound, music, haptic, and accessibility preferences. Guest progress is stored on the device. If you enable an available cloud-save option, progress may be stored using the platform provider's cloud service, such as Apple iCloud, so that it can be restored on compatible devices.

Advertising

The free version of Prism Pour may display banner and interstitial advertisements supplied by Google AdMob. Google and its partners may process information such as device identifiers, IP address, general device information, ad interactions, consent choices, and diagnostic information to deliver, limit, secure, and measure advertisements. Where required, Prism Pour or the advertising provider will request consent before using information for personalized advertising or tracking. You can purchase the Prism Supporter Pack to remove advertisements.

In-app purchases

Optional purchases are processed by Google Play or Apple's App Store. quiteloopgames does not receive your complete payment-card information. The store provides transaction and entitlement information needed to complete and restore purchases.

Device permissions

Prism Pour does not require access to your contacts, photographs, camera, microphone, or personal files. Internet access is used for advertisements, purchases, optional account and cloud features, service availability, and related security or diagnostics. Core puzzle gameplay remains available without creating an account.

Third-party services

Prism Pour may use services supplied by Apple, Google Play, Google AdMob, and related platform providers. Their handling of information is governed by their own privacy policies:

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • Apple Privacy Policy

Children's privacy

Prism Pour is intended for a general audience and is not directed primarily at children under 13. quiteloopgames does not knowingly collect personal information from children. Advertising and consent behavior may vary according to age, region, and platform requirements.

Data retention and deletion

Local guest progress remains until you clear the app's storage or uninstall it. Cloud progress and purchase records may be retained by the relevant platform provider according to its policies. You can sign out from optional account features where available. For questions or requests concerning information controlled by quiteloopgames, contact us using the address below.

Security

quiteloopgames uses reasonable measures to protect information handled by Prism Pour. No electronic system or transmission method can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated when Prism Pour's features, service providers, or data practices change. The updated policy will be published on this page with a revised effective date.

Contact

For privacy questions, support, or deletion requests, contact quiteloopgames@gmail.com.
